## What is Hermes?

**Hermes** is an AI agent platform designed to:

- Orchestrate AI workflows across multiple agents
- Handle complex tasks with autonomous decision-making
- Integrate with existing tools (GitHub, Slack, etc.)
- Provide transparent, auditable AI operations

Currently, Hermes exists as [X](link-to-existing-hermes). This migration will bring Hermes to OpsDex with improved:

- Agent orchestration
- Workflow management
- Integration capabilities
- Documentation and tooling
